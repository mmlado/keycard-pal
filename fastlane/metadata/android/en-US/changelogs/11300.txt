Upgraded to React Native 0.87. Release builds are smaller: R8 optimization and resource shrinking are on, and bundled token logos are stored at the size they are drawn. The app now draws behind the system bars on every Android version, with its bar icons always light. Menus, the scan progress bar and the card prompts keep clear of the system bars. Text fields in Settings, on Set card name and in the pairing prompt stay above the keyboard. The Inter font is bundled again.
