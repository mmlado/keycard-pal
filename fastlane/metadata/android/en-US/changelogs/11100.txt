Keycards with applet 4.0 are now supported, alongside 3.x. A new Keycards in use setting leaves out the entries and taps your cards do not need.

Bitcoin: a PSBT asking to sign with anything but SIGHASH_ALL is now refused, and an output is called change only once your Keycard confirms it.

WalletConnect, in the online build, now needs your own Project ID, entered in Settings.

Release APKs now build byte for byte the same on any machine.
