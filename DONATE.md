project maintained and open-source.

The same addresses are shown on the app's About screen, where they can be copied or
scanned as a QR code.

## Nothing is given in return

Donations are entirely voluntary. Nothing in the app is unlocked, changed or promised in
return, and there are no tiers, perks, badges or supporter lists. Every feature works the
same whether you donate or not.

## What a donation reveals

This is a property of public blockchains rather than of this app: a transfer is visible to
anyone and permanent. Because the same address is shown to everyone, donations sent to it
can be seen alongside each other. Send from an address you are comfortable having linked
to the donation.

The app collects nothing and sends nothing when you copy an address. See the
[privacy policy](https://fdroid.keycardpal.com/privacy.html) for the full picture.
